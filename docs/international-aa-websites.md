# International A.A. Websites

These links connect to Alcoholics Anonymous (A.A.) organizations, service resources, and publications in different countries and regions. Choose a destination for information about its stated location or scope. Meeting information, services, and languages vary by site; the Canada link below is for Toronto.

- <a href="https://aa-intergroup.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Online Intergroup of A.A.</a> — The Online Intergroup of Alcoholics Anonymous is a central source of information about online A.A. around the world. The OIAA uses the Internet to carry A.A.'s message of hope and recovery to the alcoholic who still suffers. The OIAA promotes unity among online A.A. groups through workshops, forums, and assemblies, responding to the needs of the Fellowship through various service committees.
- <a href="https://www.aa.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener">A.A. World Services and the General Service Office</a> — A.A. is available around the world in various countries and languages. Today, an A.A. presence can be found in approximately 180 nations worldwide. A.A. literature has been translated into over 100 languages.
- <a href="https://www.alcoholics-anonymous.org.uk" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Alcoholics Anonymous in Great Britain</a> — A.A. was established in Great Britain in 1947, beginning in London and growing steadily across England, Scotland, and Wales.
- <a href="https://aa.org.au" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Alcoholics Anonymous Australia</a> — There are A.A. meetings all over Australia. Click here to find the one nearest to you. To contact Alcoholics Anonymous Australia call 1300 222 222
- <a href="https://aa.org.nz" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Alcoholics Anonymous in Aotearoa New Zealand</a> — A.A. started in New Zealand in 1946, introduced by returning servicemen and quickly spreading across the islands.
- <a href="https://www.aatoronto.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Alcoholics Anonymous in Toronto, Canada</a> — A.A. arrived in Canada in 1940, with early groups forming in Toronto and expanding nationwide through strong local sponsorship and translated literature.
- <a href="https://alcoholics-anonymous.eu" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Alcoholics Anonymous in the Continental European Region</a> — Explore A.A. information for the Continental European Region.
- <a href="https://anonyme-alkoholiker.de" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Alcoholics Anonymous in Germany</a> — A.A. took root in Germany in the early 1950s, with early groups forming in major cities and supported by translated literature and international contact.
- <a href="https://www.alcoholicos-anonimos.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Alcoholics Anonymous in Spain</a> — A.A. emerged in Spain in the 1950s–60s, growing from small early groups into a nationwide fellowship supported by Spanish‑language literature.
- <a href="https://aatokyo.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Alcoholics Anonymous in Japan</a> — A.A. was founded in Japan in the 1960s, beginning in Tokyo and expanding nationwide through careful translation and local service work.
- <a href="https://alcoholicsanonymous.ie" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Alcoholics Anonymous in Ireland</a> — A.A. began in Ireland in the late 1940s, spreading quickly from Dublin as the fellowship’s message reached communities seeking a new path to recovery.
- <a href="https://aamexico.org.mx" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Alcoholics Anonymous in Mexico</a> — Visit for A.A. information related to Mexico.
- <a href="https://aa.org.ar" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Alcoholics Anonymous in Argentina</a> — Find A.A. information related to Argentina.
- <a href="https://aasouthafrica.org.za" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Alcoholics Anonymous in South Africa</a> — Visit for A.A. information related to South Africa.
- <a href="https://www.aagsoindia.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Alcoholics Anonymous in India</a> — AA’s message of recovery reached India in the mid‑20th century, where early groups formed in major cities and steadily grew into a nationwide Fellowship that now supports thousands seeking sobriety through the Twelve Steps.
- <a href="https://area58district33.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Oregon (USA) Area 58, Online District 33</a> — District 33 is an online service district that serves the international online A.A. community.  It is connected to the General Service Conference throuh Oregon Area 58.
- <a href="https://aamaroc.net" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Alcoholics Anonymous in Morocco</a> — A.A. began in Morocco in the mid-20th century, with early groups forming in Casablanca and serving both local and international communities.
- <a href="https://www.aagrapevine.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener">A.A. Grapevine</a> — Read the international A.A. journal and member stories.
- <a href="https://aavirtualarea.org.au" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Virtual General Service Area of Alcoholics Anonymous</a> — Explore information about this virtual A.A. service area.
